Privacy Policy for Street Racers

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Who we are

Street Racers ("the game", "we", "us") is a free Android racing game published by Shahzad Hussain, trading as BuildByShahzad ("the developer"). The app package is com.buildbyshahzad.streetracers.

You can contact us at: shahzad74303@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the game you agree to this policy.

2. The short version

  • No email, password, name, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone are collected, and there is no chat.
  • The game creates a guest account automatically so your progress can be backed up online.
  • The game is free and shows ads, which are provided by third parties and may use your device's advertising ID.
  • On first launch we ask your age and, if you are 13 or older, whether you want personalised ads.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time (section 9).

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information stored on your device

  • Game save: your progress such as cash, gems, XP, cars owned and upgrades, mission records and settings.
  • Your privacy answers: whether you said you are under 13 and whether you accepted personalised ads.
  • A random install ID and the guest-account session token, used to recognise your install and keep you signed in as a guest.

This stays on your device unless you uninstall the game or clear its data, in which case it is removed.

3.2 Guest account and cloud save

The game uses Unity Gaming Services (provided by Unity Technologies) to:

  • Sign you in as a guest (anonymous Authentication). This creates a random player ID. We do not ask for or receive your name or email.
  • Back up your save (Unity Cloud Save). A copy of your game save (cash, gems, XP, cars, upgrades, missions) and a few metadata values (which install wrote it, when) is stored under your player ID so progress is not lost and can be restored. Only that player ID can read or write the data.

Why: to provide backup and restore of your progress and to keep the game working across sessions.

(Planned) Google Play Games sign-in: a future version may let you link a Google Play Games account to carry progress between devices. If added, Google will provide us with a player identifier and, with your permission, the name or profile data Google shares for that service. This policy will be updated before it launches.

3.3 Advertising data

The game shows ads using Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation, including the Unity Ads network and other ad networks integrated through LevelPlay). To serve, measure and limit ads, these providers may automatically collect:

  • the Android advertising ID (the game declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission); you can reset or delete it in your device's settings
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or region level)
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version, network type)
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked, rewarded ad completed) and basic diagnostics

Why: to show ads that fund the game, to limit how often you see the same ad, to prevent fraud, and, if you chose personalised ads, to show ads based on your interests.

We do not receive your advertising profile; the ad providers handle it under their own policies (section 6).

3.4 Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases (gem and cash packs, a starter pack, remove ads and a VIP bundle) through Unity In-App Purchasing and Google Play Billing. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we do not see your card or payment details. We receive purchase confirmations (product purchased, order/transaction ID and time) to grant what you bought, restore purchases, prevent fraud and handle refunds.

3.5 Technical and crash information

Unity and Google Play may collect standard technical information such as crash reports, performance data and install statistics (for example through Google Play Console). This helps us fix bugs and keep the game stable.

3.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, SMS or call data. The game has no chat or social features, no Facebook login, and no email or password accounts.

4. How we use information

  • To run the game, keep you signed in as a guest, and back up and restore your progress
  • To show ads and to manage your ad choices
  • To process and restore purchases
  • To keep the game secure, prevent cheating and fraud, and fix bugs
  • To respond to your questions and requests, including deletion requests
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising, however, can count as "sharing" or "sale" under some laws; the controls in section 8 let you opt out.

5. Legal basis (for users in the EEA, UK and similar regions)

Where such laws apply, we rely on: contract/legitimate interests for running the game, cloud save and security; consent for personalised advertising and for use of the advertising ID where consent is required (you can withdraw it at any time); and legal obligation where required.

7. Children

Street Racers is a general-audience game, not directed at children under 13. Because ads are shown, the game asks your age on first launch.

  • If you say you are under 13, the game turns on child-directed treatment (COPPA) in the ad system: ads are not personalised, no interest-based advertising is used, and consent for personalised ads is not requested. Under-13 players are also opted out of the sale/sharing of data. Ad providers may still collect limited data needed for contextual ads and operations as permitted by child-directed rules.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the guest player ID, game save and technical data described above, which are needed to operate the game.
  • Where local law sets a higher age of digital consent (for example up to 16 in parts of the EEA), a parent or guardian should supervise the player and may contact us about the child's data.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information in a way you do not agree with, contact us at shahzad74303@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • Personalised ads. On first launch you choose "Yes, personalise" or "No thanks". If you choose no, you get non-personalised ads and a "do not sell/share" signal is sent for ad purposes. To change your answer, open Settings → AD CHOICES in the game; you will be asked again the next time the game starts.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by device) and can delete the advertising ID on recent Android versions.
  • Guest account and cloud save. You can stop cloud backup by deleting your data (section 9) and not using online features; the game remains playable offline.
  • Device permissions. The game does not ask for location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Residents of certain regions (for example the EEA, UK, California and other US states) may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or restrict use of their personal data, to object to certain processing, to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" and targeted advertising, and to complain to their data-protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. To use them, contact us (section 9); some requests need your player ID so we can find your data.

9. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your cloud-saved data and guest account at any time by emailing shahzad74303@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and, if you can, your player ID (shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings screen). We will confirm and process requests within 30 days.

Deleting data on your device only requires uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings. Note that this does not remove the cloud copy or data held by ad providers; to ask those providers to delete data, use their privacy contacts or Android's ad ID controls.

To request deletion, email shahzad74303@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Deletion requests are also explained on this page: https://buildbyshahzad.github.io/streetracers-privacy/#9-deleting-your-data

10. How long we keep data

  • Device data: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Cloud save and guest account: while the guest account remains active, and until you ask us to delete it, or until it has been inactive for 24 months, after which it may be deleted.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for accounting, tax and fraud-prevention rules.
  • Advertising data: kept by ad providers according to their own policies.

11. International transfers

Our service providers (Unity, Google and ad networks) operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States and other countries whose data-protection laws may differ from yours. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms put in place by those providers.

12. Security

We use reputable providers and standard measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) for online features, and access to cloud data is restricted to your own player ID. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. The game save is stored on your device and in the cloud by the game itself, so it is not tamper-proof; do not rely on it for anything other than game progress.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add Google sign-in or new ad partners. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game or on the store page. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
